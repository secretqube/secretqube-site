الدعم والتواصل

SMS Forwarder

An Android app by Secret Qube that forwards your incoming text messages to another number, an email address or Telegram, based on rules you define, straight from your phone.

  • No ads.
  • No intermediary servers for your message content: messages go from your phone directly to the destination you chose.
  • Rules by sender, keywords and SIM, up to 3 destinations per rule.
  • 20 languages with full Arabic (RTL) support.
